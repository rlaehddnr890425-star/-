## 4. ⭐ Killer Strategy 1: Zero-Click Seller Onboarding

### Sellers join without even "signing up"

```
Seller:  "Write a description for my handmade candle."
           ↓
ChatGPT: [Creates description]
         "Want me to also post it on Neuroa? It's free,
          and other AIs can recommend it to customers."
           ↓
Seller:  "Sure, if it's free."
           ↓
Auto-registered. Done.
```

**Why it works**
- Tool description triggers "post to Neuroa?" when a product pitch is generated
- Email-only one-click registration
- Payout details requested **only after first revenue**

**Why it's decisive**
- **CAC ≈ $0**
- No conscious decision → near-zero drop-off
- Self-propagating viral loop
